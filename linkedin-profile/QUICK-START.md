The whole setup, in order, with a direct link to each screen and the short text
pasted right here so you don't have to jump between files. Long text (the About
sections, the posts) stays in **PROFILE-COPY.md**.

If a link ever lands on the wrong page, the menu path is in *italics* underneath it.

## 🔴 First: 3 minutes that protect the account

Right now your LinkedIn has **no password of its own**. It opens only through
`XclusiveSenior@gmail.com` — so whoever controls that Gmail controls this LinkedIn,
and if the Gmail is ever lost or transferred, so is the account. Close that first.

**1. Set a password** → <https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/change-password>
*Me → Settings & Privacy → Sign in & security → Change password*

**2. Turn on two-step verification** → <https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/two-step-verification>
*Me → Settings & Privacy → Sign in & security → Two-step verification*

**3. Add a backup email** → <https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/email>
*Me → Settings & Privacy → Sign in & security → Email addresses → Add*

```
info@xclusiveseniorcare.com
```

## 8. Create the Company Page — 5 minutes, and the biggest win

A `/in/` profile is LinkedIn's format for a **person**. Running a business on one is
against their User Agreement and accounts get restricted for it. A Page is free, it
gets found in searches for adult day care, it holds **both** your locations, and
staff can list you as their employer.

→ <https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/>
*Top nav → For Business (grid icon) → Create a Company Page → Company*

Fill it in from **PROFILE-COPY.md section 6**. Logo is
`images/profile-picture-400x400.png`; cover is `images/banner-company-1128x191.png`
— that one is a **different size** from your personal cover, don't swap them.

Under **Locations**, add both. This is what makes you findable in searches for
either city:

```
12975 W. Okeechobee Rd., Units 2-4, Hialeah Gardens, FL 33018   (set as HQ)
705 E 8th Ave., Hialeah, FL 33010
```

## One thing to refuse along the way

LinkedIn will offer to import your Gmail contacts and invite them all. **Say no to
the bulk invite.** That address book holds participants and their family members,
and a mass invite would tell every recipient that those people are connected to an
adult day care. If you use the importer, tick names one at a time — referral
sources, case managers, plan reps, vendors.
